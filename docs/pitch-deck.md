## Slides

### 1. Brand & Vision
- **What to say:** RoHub creates retreats that combine rural accommodation, local gastronomy, nature, tastings, visits to local producers, traditional activities and free time — a complete, flexible experience, unlike competitors tied to a single fixed location.
- **Photo:** #1

### 2. The Problem
- **What to say:** Most retreats are tied to a single location with the same activities. Transport, accommodation and activities are managed separately, consuming time and resources. Planning is stressful. 38% of employees in Romania experienced high stress the previous day (Gallup, 2025).
- **Photo:** #2 or #3

### 3. The Solution & Target Market
- **What to say:** RoHub connects three existing realities: Stress (38% high-stress employees), Benefits (1,170 RON average extra-salary budget per employee in 2026), Demand (27% of tourism is in the top categories of important benefits in 2025). Expansion signal: 4.51 billion euros spent by Romanian residents on domestic travel in 2024.
- **Photo:** #4
